About EDENFALL

EDENFALL is a fantasy-inspired
2D RPG adventure built in Java,
where exploration, combat, and
mystery come together in a vast
procedurally generated world.


Created by John Clark Melitar,
this game features
journey through dangerous forests,
forgotten ruins, ancient dungeons,
and lands corrupted by unknown forces.
Fight powerful monsters,
discover hidden secrets,
collect rare equipment,
and grow stronger as you uncover
the truth behind the fallen world.


Choose from unique classes with
different weapons, abilities,
and playstyles — from swift archers
and powerful mages to cursed warriors
and mysterious beings tied to
the darkness itself.


Every adventure in EDENFALL
is different. Explore endlessly
generated environments, battle
dangerous enemies, upgrade your gear,
and survive in a world shaped by
ancient magic and lost history.

CHARACTER CLASSES

ROGUE

A lightning-fast assassin with unmatched agility, devastating critical hits, and a toolkit built around stealth, poison, and precision. Fragile but deadly, the Rogue punishes mistakes with lethal efficiency.

MAGE

A pure elemental nuker, the Mage channels devastating power through fire, frost, breeze, and storm. Though fragile, their destructive power is second to none, controlling the battlefield with elemental chaos.

KNIGHT

A pure brute knight, devaastating hits, and tough durability. The Knight knows basic magic, but he's equipped with mastery in melee combat, enabling his sword and shield to attack and defend.

ARCHER

A sharp-shooting attacker, capable of hitting the enemies miles away. The Archer pinpoints the enemy with accuracy and precision while maintaining caution far away.
